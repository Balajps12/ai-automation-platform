| Area | What’s missing / could be tightened |
|------|--------------------------------------|
| **Hook** | The opening line (“The Truth About LinkedIn Growth”) is vague and competes with countless similar headlines. It doesn’t promise a concrete payoff or spark curiosity quickly. |
