**Cinematic AI Video‑Generation Prompt – “Lead‑Gen Launchpad”**  
*Style: High‑energy startup commercial (30‑second cut). Aim: Instantly hook B2B decision‑makers and drive them to the sign‑up form.*

---

### 1️⃣ Opening Scene – “The Problem”
- **Environment:** Open‑plan, modern co‑working loft at sunrise. Glass walls reveal a bustling city skyline, a hint of morning fog.
- **Lighting:** Soft, cool‑blue key light streaming through the windows, accented by subtle practical LED strips on the desks. Slight rim light on the protagonist’s silhouette.
- **Camera Movement:** Slow **dolly‑in** from a wide establishing shot to a tight 2‑second push‑in on a frustrated founder (mid‑30s, casual tech‑wear) staring at a cluttered spreadsheet on a laptop.
- **Mood:** Tense, “too much data, not enough leads” atmosphere; muted color palette (steel blues, charcoal).
- **Realism Touch:** Grain‑free 4K, shallow depth of field (f/2.2), natural window reflections.

---

### 2️⃣ Transition – “The Spark”
- **Environment:** The same loft, but now a holographic UI flickers into view above the desk (animated lead‑gen dashboard).
- **Lighting:** A **warm amber “spark”** wash (gobo) sweeps across the scene, instantly changing the color temperature.
- **Camera Movement:** Quick **tilt‑up** following the hologram’s rise, then a **whip‑pan** to the founder’s eyes widening.
- **Mood:** Instant shift from frustration to curiosity‑excitement.
- **Realism:** Particle‑level light scattering on the hologram; subtle lens flare that respects physical optics.

---

### 3️⃣ Core Demo – “The Platform in Action”
- **Environment:** Split‑screen virtual environment:
  - **Left side:** Real‑world office – the founder clicks a “Generate Leads” button on the laptop.
  - **Right side:** Inside the software – 3‑D animated data streams (neon lines) flow into a sleek “Lead Funnel” UI, turning into contact cards that pop up with company logos, names, and scores.
- **Lighting:** Dual‑tone: left half remains cool blue; right half bursts in vibrant teal‑green with dynamic back‑lighting from the UI.
- **Camera Movement:** **Seamless cross‑fade** to a **smooth 3‑axis orbit** around the UI, revealing layers (AI engine, verification, enrichment). Then a **rack focus** pulling from the UI back to the founder’s satisfied smile.
- **Mood:** Confidence‑building, “this is how we win.”
- **Realism:** High‑poly 3‑D assets with physically‑based rendering (PBR). UI elements obey real‑world refraction and subtle subsurface scattering.

---

### 4️⃣ Social Proof – “Happy Customers”
- **Environment:** Montage of three micro‑scenes (each 2 seconds) shot in different real locations: a coffee‑shop startup, a sleek corporate boardroom, a home‑office with a pet cat. Each shows a different professional (female engineer, senior marketer, indie founder) glancing at the same lead‑gen dashboard on a tablet/phone, giving a quick nod or fist‑pump.
- **Lighting:** Natural daylight for coffee‑shop, soft diffused overhead for boardroom, warm home lighting for the home office – all **color‑graded** with a unified teal‑orange cinematic split‑tone.
- **Camera Movement:** Quick **snap‑zoom** on each device screen, then a brief **push‑in** on the smiling face.
- **Mood:** Trust, community, diversity.
- **Realism:** Real‑world texture mapping on coffee cups, wood table, corporate glass; depth of field that matches lens focal length (50mm).

---

### 5️⃣ Call‑to‑Action – “Join the Wave”
- **Environment:** Back to the original loft, now fully lit with sunrise turning to bright morning. The founder stands beside a transparent floor‑to‑ceiling glass wall; outside, a **digital wave of data points** surges across the cityscape (visual metaphor for leads flowing in).
- **Lighting:** High‑key, golden hour sunlight pours in, balanced with cool back‑light from the data wave.
- **Camera Movement:** Start with a **low-angle crane up** showing the wave, then **smooth dolly‑track** forward to the founder who points to a bold, animated “GET STARTED” button that appears on the screen beside him.
- **Mood:** Aspirational, empowering, “the future is yours.”
- **Realism:** Real-time particle physics for the data wave, subtle motion blur on the crane move, authentic reflections in the glass.

---

### 6️⃣ End Card
- **Background:** Dark‑mode UI backdrop with subtle gradient.
- **Text:** “Turn clicks into customers. 10 × faster. Start your free trial now.” – bold sans‑serif, white typography.
- **Logo + URL:** Centered, with a soft **pulse** animation.
- **Lighting:** Minimal rim light on the text, gentle vignette.

---

## Technical Parameters for AI Renderer
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Resolution** | 4K (3840 × 2160) |
| **Frame Rate** | 30 fps (cinematic motion blur) |
| **Aspect Ratio** | 16:9 |
| **Camera Lenses** | 24mm (wide) → 50mm (portrait) → 85mm (dramatic) |
| **Depth of Field** | Variable, f/2.0–f/5.6 per shot |
| **Lighting Runtime** | HDRI sky + practical LEDs + volumetric rays |
| **Render Engine** | Physically‑Based (Unreal Engine 5 / Octane) |
| **Post‑Process** | Color‑grade teal‑orange split tone, film grain 0.2%, slight vignette, lens flare presets |
| **Audio Cue (optional)** | Rising synth pad → crisp UI clicks → upbeat electronic groove ending on a “ding”. |
