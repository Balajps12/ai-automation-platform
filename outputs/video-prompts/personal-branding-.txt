| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Scene** | A black‑and‑white office at dawn; a desk, a laptop, a single coffee mug. A young entrepreneur (the brand protagonist) opens the laptop, the screen flickering to life. |
| **Camera movement** | **Slow dolly in** from a static wide shot to a tight close‑up on the protagonist’s eyes as the screen lights up. |
| **Lighting** | **Low‑key back‑lighting** – a single rising sun or office lamp casts a rim light, creating a subtle silhouette behind the subject. |
| **Environment** | Minimalist, clutter‑free workspace; a small plant adds a touch of life. |
| **Mood** | Introspective, hopeful. |
| **Realism** | Natural skin tones, subtle motion blur to show real-time “just‑started” moments. |

## 🎬 Overall Cinematic Blueprint

| Parameter | What to Encode in AI Prompt |
|---|---|
| **Style** | Startup Commercial – modern, sleek, inspirational. |
| **Camera language** | Combine dolly, orbit, hand‑held, time‑lapse, and steadicam crane for dynamic storytelling. |
| **Lighting palette** | Low‑key to high‑key mixtures; rim lighting for heroes; LED wash for environment. |
| **Environment Cues** | Minimalist office → collaborative co‑work → high‑tech lab → launch stage → studio. |
| **Mood progression** | From contemplative spark → collaborative excitement → triumphant breakthrough → celebratory launch → visionary climax. |
| **Realism guidelines** | Use photoreal materials, physically accurate lighting, natural motion blur, realistic sound overlays; keep the A.I. artistry subtle enough to preserve authenticity. |
