**Cinematic AI‑Video Prompt: “Email Automation – The Future, Simplified”**

---

### 1. Scene Layout (3‑Act Structure)

| Act | Scene | Duration | Key Visual Elements |
|-----|-------|----------|---------------------|
| **Act 1 – The Problem** | 0:00–0:15 | A cluttered office, emails flooding a screen, a stressed manager. | Desk overflowing, stack of unopened mail, blue LED notification counter rising. |
| **Act 2 – The Solution** | 0:15–0:45 | Seamless transition to a sleek, cloud‑based dashboard. User selects “Automate” and the UI triggers a “Email Flow Engine” animation. | Dark‑mode UI, floating gear icon, cascading email icons turning into a smooth pipeline. |
| **Act 3 – The Result** | 0:45–1:00 | A calm, productive workspace: team collaborating, charts growing, inbox icon showing zero notifications. | Bright, minimal home office, graph lines shooting up, glowing email icon with a checkmark. |

---

### 2. Camera Movement

| Act | Movement | Shot type | Purpose |
|-----|----------|-----------|---------|
| **Act 1** | *Handheld close‑up* | 1‑2 hand‑held pans across the inbox counter | Conveys urgency, chaos |
| **Act 2** | *Smooth dolly/reverse dolly* | Medium shot morphing into a tight UI close‑up | Highlights technological elegance |
| **Act 3** | *Steadicam sweep* | Wide shot through the room, ending on a team high‑five | Demonstrates collaboration and calm |

---

### 3. Lighting

| Act | Lighting Style | Color Palette | Mood |
|-----|----------------|---------------|------|
| **Act 1** | Harsh cool LED strip, high‑contrast | Blue/white with pops of orange from notification alerts | Tense, overwhelmed |
| **Act 2** | Soft, diffused direction from above, subtle back‑light | Midnight blue with silver accents | Optimistic, futuristic |
| **Act 3** | Warm, ambient hue (soft amber) | Golden yellows, pastel office tones | Invigorating, hopeful |

### 4. Environment

- **Act 1**:  
  *Environment* – Mid‑20th‑century office, heavy desks, analog clocks ticking.  
  *Props* – Fax machine, coffee mug, stack of Post‑it notes.

- **Act 2**:  
  *Environment* – Transforms into a clean, cloud‑centric data center.  
  *Props* – Virtual desktop, holographic workflow diagram, sleek laptop.

- **Act 3**:  
  *Environment* – Modern, open‑plan office with glass walls and greenery.  
  *Props* – Touchscreen KPI board, ergonomic chairs, laptop with “All‑Done” badge.

---

### 5. Mood & Tone

- **Act 1** – **Stressful** & **overwhelmed**  
- **Act 2** – **Curiosity** & **elegance**  
- **Act 3** – **Empowerment** & **clarity**

---

### 6. Realism & Render Quality

- **Render Engine** – Unreal Engine 5 with Nanite for ultra‑realistic geometry, Ray‑Tracing for global illumination.  
- **Textures** – High‑resolution, physically‑based materials (metal, glass, fabric).  
- **Focal Depth** – Shallow depth of field on UI objects; gentle bokeh on background employees.  
- **Post‑Processing** – Color grading using DaVinci Resolve LUT to give a polished “startup” vibe.

---

### 7. Suggested Audio Beats

- **Soundtrack** – Upbeat, mid‑tempo electronic track.  
- **Sound FX** – Email “ding”, typing, gentle wind‑up for automation, triumphant chime for final “All‑Done”.  
- **Voice‑over** – Crisp, friendly tone: “Say goodbye to inbox overload. Automate, scale, succeed.”
