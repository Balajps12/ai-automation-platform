**[TikTok/Reels‑style UGC video – 45‑60 sec]**  

---

### 🎬 1️⃣ HOOK (0‑5 sec)  
*Camera is selfie‑style, bright background, energetic vibe.*  

**Creator (big smile, eyes wide):**  
“Stop scrolling! 🚀 If you’re tired of being *just another face* in the feed, you need ONE brand trick right now!”  

*(Quick cut to a text overlay that flashes: “💥 3‑second brand boost”)*
  

### 🎬 2️⃣ PROBLEM (5‑15 sec)  
*Switch to a slightly “meh” clip of the creator scrolling through endless profiles, looking frustrated.*  

**Voice‑over (same creator, sigh):**  
“Your posts get likes, but nobody knows *who* you really are. 🤷‍♀️ You’re posting great content, yet the algorithm keeps treating you like a ghost. No offers, no collaborations, no “who are you?” messages.”  

*(On‑screen text: “🤦‍♂️ 0 DM’s, 0 gigs, 0 identity”)*
  

### 🎬 3️⃣ SOLUTION (15‑40 sec)  
*Music ramps up. Creator points to the camera, then flips to a clean whiteboard/phone screen showing the 3‑step framework.*  

**Creator (excited):**  
“Meet the **3‑Step Personal Brand Blueprint** that turned my side‑hustle into a full‑time client magnet—*in just 7 days*!”  

1️⃣ **Define Your Signature** – 1 sentence that says *exactly* what you solve. *(Shows creator writing “I help busy professionals look & feel confident in 10 min a day.”)*  

2️⃣ **Visual Hook** – Pick ONE color + ONE font + ONE vibe and use it everywhere. *(Clip of creator swapping phone themes, logo, thumbnails.)*  

3️⃣ **Story‑First Content** – Every post starts with a mini‑story that ends with a CTA. *(Shows a quick before/after of a Reel caption.)*  

**Creator (pointing at the phone):**  
“Drop a comment ‘🚀’ if you want the free PDF that walks you through each step—plus a swipe‑up link to my 5‑minute brand audit.”  

*(Screen split: PDF cover + “Free Now!” badge.)*  

### 🎬 4️⃣ CTA (40‑55 sec)  
*Creator leans in, playful wink.*  

**Creator:**  
“Your brand is the only thing that can’t be stolen. 🎯 Tap the link in my bio, grab the free guide, and let’s turn those likes into *real* opportunities. Ready? Let’s go!”  

*(Ends with a fast‑cut montage of the creator receiving DMs, booking calls, and a final text overlay: “💡 Brand Bold. Brand You. #PersonalBranding”)*  

---  

**Quick Production Tips:**  
- Keep shots under 3 seconds, rapid cuts.  
- Use on‑screen captions for every spoken line (TikTok’s algorithm loves them).  
- Add upbeat, royalty‑free track that builds on the beat.  
- End with a clear, clickable link or “link in bio” CTA.
